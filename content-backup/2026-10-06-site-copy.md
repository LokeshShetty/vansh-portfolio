## How to restore

The source at that point is commit `6982102`, also kept on the branch
`backup/content-2026-10-06` on GitHub.

- See every change since: `git diff backup/content-2026-10-06 -- src`
- Restore all site content: `git checkout backup/content-2026-10-06 -- src`
  then commit
- Undo just the content commit: `git revert <content commit>`
